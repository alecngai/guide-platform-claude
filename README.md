# Guide Platform for Claude

One plugin that connects Claude to Guide Platform, for Alpha staff.

## What it does

- Connects your Claude to Guide Platform so it can read your own kid list, what is done each week, and (when you are allowed) read and save your notes.
- Adds one recipe: "notes by pillar" for a kid.

## Who can use it

- Alpha staff, from Claude on your school login. A personal Claude account may not connect.
- You sign in with your school Google account the first time, and the Guide Platform team allows each person by name.

## What is in this repository

Three small text files: the plugin list, the plugin card, and the Guide Platform connector address. No passwords and no student data.

## Help

How to connect and how to save notes are in Guide Platform under Help: search "connect my AI".
Questions: #guide-platform-support.
